Artist Profile Miro Sounds

Liam Lowe

DJ & Producer · London
Intro Videos Mixes
mirosounds.com

Liam has been DJing around the world for 15 years, acquiring the finest record collection for every party, from weeklong weddings to late night clubs. Blending feel-good classics with unexpected discoveries to keep the dancefloor pumping. He’s recently spent 2 years playing with Groove Armada and regularly partners with brands like Ralph Lauren. His production work has included collaborations with Bryan Ferry, Noel Gallagher and Nile Rodgers.

Signature Mixes

Recent Event Mixes

Recent mixes, from private events to club nights, showing different directions Liam can take a party, from nostalgic singalongs to a more adventurous feel.

90s & 00s Vocal House Classics

An upbeat mix of vocal house and dance-pop classics from the 90s and early 2000s, featuring Kylie Minogue, Robin S., Madonna and CeCe Peniston. Familiar hooks, euphoric melodies and singalong favourites give it a nostalgic, feel-good party atmosphere.

Soul, Funk & Disco

A feel-good blend of soul, funk and disco that builds into big pop-dance favourites. From The O’Jays, Aretha Franklin and Tim Maia through Donna Summer and Gloria Gaynor to ABBA, Madonna and Gala, it’s a warm, groove-led selection full of familiar hooks and singalong moments.

Eclectic Club Sounds

An adventurous electronic mix balancing familiar vocal hooks with underground house and left-field club selections. Madonna, Yaeji and The Shapeshifters provide recognisable moments, while KiNK, Cinthie and Aphex Twin bring a more exploratory, club-focused edge.

Liam is happy to work from a brief and to connect directly over the phone ahead of an event. He’s also always up for expanding the performance, adding live sax, percussion or vocals to his set.
